Senior Locating

About

Senior Locating helps seniors and their families find the right senior living community across Southern California, including Los Angeles and Orange County. Their advisors take the time to understand each family's care needs, budget, lifestyle, and preferred location before recommending suitable options.

From independent living and assisted living to memory care, Senior Locating provides personalized community matching, in-person and virtual tours, and hands-on support throughout the placement process. Families can learn more at https://seniorlocating.com. Because communities pay for the placement service, guidance is provided at no cost to families.

FAQ

What types of senior living communities can you help us find?

Senior Locating helps families find independent living, assisted living, and memory care communities based on their loved one's needs and preferences.

Does Senior Locating charge families?

No. The service is paid for by participating senior living communities, so families receive guidance at no cost.

Can you help with community tours?

Yes. Senior Locating can help arrange in-person or virtual tours and provide support throughout the placement process.
